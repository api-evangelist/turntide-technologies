The instructions in this guide are intended for a mechanical technician who is familiar working with Commercial air handling units (AHUs) This guide provides instructions on how to install and set up the Turntide Motor System. consisting of the Turntide Smart Motor and the Turntide Motor Controller. The contents of kits and recommended tools are also listed.
